A structured prompt system for creating high-engagement carousel posts on social media.

**Core Principle:** Every carousel must feel like a story unfolding—using curiosity gaps, emotional triggers, and open loops to keep readers swiping.

**10-Slide Structure:**
1. **Hook** — Bold, controversial statement (5–10 words max) that creates pattern interrupt
2. **Rehook** — Tease outcome without answering; build curiosity gap
3. **Relatable Pain/Story Start** — Use "Most people think," "I used to," or "Everyone does this wrong"
4–7. **Value** — Deliver main content through narrative; one key idea per slide with short, punchy sentences
8. **Turning Point** — Reveal the key insight or "aha moment"
9. **Actionable Takeaway** — Provide clear, practical steps for immediate application
10. **CTA** — Strong call-to-action ("Comment X," "Follow for more," etc.)

**Psychological Triggers to Deploy:**
- Curiosity gap and pattern interrupt
- Social proof tone
- FOMO (fear of missing out)
- Contrarian ideas
- Fast rewards/quick wins

**Writing Style:**
Short, sharp sentences with zero fluff. Use conversational, slightly dramatic tone as if speaking to one person. Every line should create momentum.

**Output Requirements:**
Clearly label each slide with 1–2 lines max per slide. Format ready-to-post with no explanations. Goal: reader swipes every slide, feels something, learns value, and takes action.
