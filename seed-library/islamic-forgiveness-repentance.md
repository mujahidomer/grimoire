## Summary
This short Islamic lecture excerpt describes Quranic passages about Jahannam (Hell) and events on the Day of Judgment, specifically focusing on the moment when Hell is brought forward and all creation witnesses it, leading to universal prostration before Allah.

## Key Takeaways
- **key_message:** Regardless of one's faith status in this life, all of creation will be humbled and powerless before the reality of the Day of Judgment
- **quranic_concepts:**
  - 4.9 billion angels will be dragging Jahannam forward on the Day of Judgment
  - Jahannam was created solely for the purpose of punishing humanity
  - When Jahannam first sees human beings, it will burst out of anger
  - All living things—believers and non-believers alike—will fall into prostration in fear when they witness Jahannam
  - Allah will command creation to stand up, declaring that the Day of Judgment is not a day of worship but of judgment
  - Belief or disbelief on Earth will have no value on that day
