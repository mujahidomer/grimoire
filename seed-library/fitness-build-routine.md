## Summary
Coach Kozak from Hasfit guides viewers through a 15-minute total body strength workout using light dumbbells. The workout consists of nine exercises performed in three rounds, each lasting 30 seconds, designed for beginners with minimal equipment requirements.

## Key Takeaways
- **equipment_needed:** Light dumbbells (males: 5-10 lbs, females: 3-8 lbs) and optional mat
- **workout_structure:** Three complete rounds of nine exercises, 30 seconds per exercise
- **exercises_in_order:**
  - Seesaw row (bent over at 45 degrees, alternating arms, elbows in)
  - Goblet squat (one dumbbell, feet shoulder-width apart, butt back first)
  - Seesaw shoulder press (alternating arms, slight knee bend, full range of motion)
  - Calf raise (up on balls of feet, controlled movement)
  - Alternating dumbbell curl (curl pinky in as you come up, full range)
  - Lying chest press (dumbbells up and down, gentle ground contact)
  - Lying knee raise (hands under butt for lower back support, feet up when possible)
  - Romanian deadlift (RDL) (butt back, chest forward, straight back, mid-shin depth)
  - Overhead tricep extension (diamond grip, elbows in, both arms equally)
- **upright_row:** Dumbbell upright row (elbows high not hands, shoulders back, great for upper back and traps)
- **form_cues:**
  - Keep back straight, not rolled over
  - Retract shoulders throughout movements
  - Keep elbows in on rows and presses
  - Weight in hips, not knees on squats
  - Maintain controlled movement, no bouncing
  - Breathe throughout all movements
  - Keep head up to maintain straight back on RDL
- **modifications:** If new to exercise or unable to go full depth, it's okay to modify range of motion but maintain proper form
- **recommended_progression:** Consistent daily practice will yield results; check Hasfit website for 30 and 90-day programs
