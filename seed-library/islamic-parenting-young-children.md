## Summary
This content critiques the common parenting tactic of using fear of parents (or threats like 'I'll tell Mom/Dad') to control children ages 0-7. The speaker argues this approach is ineffective because children will eventually be unsupervised, and advocates instead for teaching children to develop a connection with and understanding of Allah as their primary source of guidance and accountability.
