## Summary
This content critiques the common parenting tactic of using fear of parents (e.g., threatening to call mom or dad) to discipline young children ages 0-7. It argues that this approach is ineffective and advocates instead for teaching children to connect with and fear Allah, establishing a foundation of spiritual accountability that will guide them when parents aren't present.

## Key Takeaways
- Fear-based parenting that relies on threatening to involve the other parent is ultimately ineffective
- Children will eventually find themselves in situations without parental supervision, requiring internal moral guidance
- Teaching Allah-consciousness and spiritual accountability is more valuable than fostering fear of parental authority
- The ages 0-7 are a critical window for establishing a child's relationship with spiritual values
- True discipline comes from internalized values rather than external threats
