## Transcript
Let's make chicken karahi according to science. Today we're making Pakistan's beloved dish. Bohot Mazraa. According to science. Let's start with the spices. We want to toast the whole spices because pre-ground spices have been exposed to oxygen for weeks or even months. So the terpenes, which are the flavor compounds, evaporate, losing flavor. And toasting whole spices evaporates any excess moisture concentrating the flavor. Then we want to lightly crush. It's really important that we lightly crush here as ground spices can burn during the boonah stage. Next, the chicken. Butcher your chicken into 12 to 16 pieces, i.e. the karahi cut. And then sear on all sides. We are aiming for Mayyad flavor chemistry here. And also, the bone-in gives the masala a lip-coating mouthfeel from the gelatin. Then fry ginger and garlic on the side, which I go into more detail on why on my sub stack to help with your attention span. Next, the tomatoes. Add half tomatoes to the pan, cut side down, and cover with a lid. Adding the tomatoes this way steams them and keeps the gravy smooth and allows the skin to separate naturally as the tomato skins contain insoluble cellulose, which are highly bitter on longer cooking. Then add spices, which I go into more detail on why on my sub stack. Now, the most important step. Here, we are trying to achieve a boonah, which literally means to fry. And it is the technique that defines the karahi. What happens is an emulsification breaking process. Here, the water quickly evaporates and the oil separates, which is a sign that your masala is almost ready. Next, the yogurt. Whiskey yogurt, which breaks down the casein network into a smooth liquid and slowly pour into your masala. This allows for further emulsification and pouring gradually raises the temperature by introducing small amounts. Then cook for a further three to four minutes to allow the casein from the yogurt and the fat to integrate. Finally, sprinkle fenugreek, garam masala, and then serve with naan, fresh chillies, lemon. And that's chicken karahi made according to science.
