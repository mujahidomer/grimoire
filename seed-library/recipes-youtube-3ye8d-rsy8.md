## Summary
An Albanian cooking channel presents three simple recipes featuring ground meat and eggs: a baked tortilla dish with ground meat and mozzarella, a pan-fried chicken breast with potatoes and ginger-soy glaze, and a baked ground meat and egg tortilla casserole. All recipes are quick and budget-friendly.

## Key Takeaways
- **recipe_1_meat_and_egg_tortilla:**
  - **ingredients:**
    - 2 eggs
    - 2 small tomatoes
    - ½ onion
    - Vegetable oil
    - 200 g ground meat
    - Parsley
    - Italian herbs
    - Red pepper
    - Dried garlic
    - Salt
    - Tortilla
    - Mozzarella
  - **cooking_steps:**
    - Add cooked ground meat to beaten eggs
    - Add ground meat to prepared mixture
    - Layer tortilla, mozzarella, tortilla, mozzarella in baking dish
    - Bake at 200°C/400°F for 15 minutes
- **recipe_2_chicken_with_ginger_soy_glaze:**
  - **ingredients:**
    - 800 g chicken breast
    - 4 small potatoes
    - ½ onion
    - 1 carrot
    - Salt
    - Red pepper
    - Black pepper
    - 1 egg
    - 2 tablespoons flour
    - Coconut
    - Oil for frying
    - 40 g butter
    - 2 garlic cloves
    - 1 tablespoon lemon juice
    - Ginger
    - 2 tablespoons soy sauce
    - 1 tablespoon honey
  - **cooking_steps:**
    - Mix egg, flour, and coconut together
    - Coat chicken pieces in egg mixture
    - Fry chicken from both sides on medium heat
    - Cook for 10 minutes on low heat in butter with garlic, lemon juice, ginger, soy sauce, and honey
- **recipe_3_ground_meat_tortilla_casserole:**
  - **ingredients:**
    - Wet onions
    - 450 g ground meat
    - 2 eggs
    - 3 tortillas
    - 1 tablespoon salt
    - Black pepper
    - Cumin
    - 100 g mozzarella cheese
  - **cooking_steps:**
    - Cut tortillas and add to bowl
    - Mix in ground meat, eggs, salt, black pepper, and cumin thoroughly
    - Add mozzarella cheese
    - Bake at 400°F/200°C for 30 minutes
