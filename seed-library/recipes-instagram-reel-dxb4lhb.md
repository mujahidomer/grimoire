## Summary
A recipe for biryani-infused butter that combines caramelized onions, garlic, mint, saffron, and biryani masala into a luxurious compound butter. The butter can be used as a simple spread on parathas or incorporated into more complex dishes like chicken wraps for spicy, aromatic flavor.

## Key Takeaways
- **ingredients:**
  - **base:**
    - 454gms unsalted butter
  - **other:**
    - 1 tbsp milk
    - salt to taste
  - **aromatics:**
    - 6-7 onions (sliced)
    - 4-5 cloves garlic
    - 2 sprigs mint
  - **spices_and_flavorings:**
    - 1 tbsp biryani masala
    - 1 tsp rose water
    - pinch of saffron
- **cooking_steps:**
  - Fry off the sliced onions until caramelized (parista)
  - Fry the garlic in the same oil
  - Chop up the mint
  - Add one tablespoon of milk to a pinch of saffron and let it bloom
  - Add the bloomed saffron and milk mixture to the softened butter
  - Mix in the fried garlic and chopped mint
  - Add one tablespoon of biryani masala
  - Add a splash of rose water
  - Mix until fully combined
- **flavor_profile:** Spicy, aromatic, addictive
- **suggested_uses:**
  - Spread on paratha
  - Use in chicken wraps
  - Simple applications or complex dishes
