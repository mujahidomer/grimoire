## Summary
A cooking tutorial showing how to prepare baked potatoes topped with chicken fillet and cheese, served with a homemade sour cream and garlic sauce. The recipe combines simple ingredients baked in stages to create a complete, delicious meal.

## Key Takeaways
- **ingredients:**
  - 3 eggs
  - 5 potatoes
  - 1 onion
  - Parsley
  - Paprika
  - Salt and black pepper
  - 2 tablespoons flour
  - Vegetable oil
  - 1 chicken fillet
  - 2 peppers
  - 2 cloves garlic
  - 50 g cheddar cheese
  - 50 g mozzarella cheese
  - 3 tablespoons sour cream
  - 1 tablespoon mayonnaise
  - 1-2 cloves garlic
  - Dill
  - A pinch of onion
- **cooking_steps:**
  - Rinse the potatoes with water
  - Mix 3 eggs together
  - Add the egg mixture to the potatoes and mix well
  - Add flour, salt, black pepper, paprika, onions, and parsley to the mixture
  - Bake in the oven at 180°C for 25 minutes
  - Cut 1 chicken fillet into 6 equal pieces
  - Chop 2 peppers and 2 cloves of garlic
  - Brown the chicken pieces with onions, peppers, and garlic in vegetable oil
  - Top the baked potatoes with the chicken mixture
  - Add cheddar cheese and mozzarella cheese on top
  - Bake in the oven at 180°C for 15 minutes
  - Mix 3 tablespoons sour cream, 1 tablespoon mayonnaise, 1-2 cloves minced garlic, dill, and a pinch of onion to make sauce
