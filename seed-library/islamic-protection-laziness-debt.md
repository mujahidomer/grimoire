## Summary
A recitation of a traditional Islamic dua (supplication) seeking Allah's protection from various hardships including sadness, grief, laziness, cowardice, miserliness, debt, and oppression. The dua is presented as a short-form video with musical background.

## Transcript
Oh Allah, I ask you to protect me from any sadness, [music] any grief, any sorrow. Oh Allah, I ask your protection from inability and laziness. I ask you, oh Allah, to protect me from being a coward and being miserly. Oh Allah, I ask you to protect me from debt overpowering me and the people overpowering me.
