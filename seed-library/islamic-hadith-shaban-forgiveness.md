## Summary
This hadith from Prophet Muhammad ﷺ describes the spiritual significance of the 15th night of Sha'bān (Laylat al-Barā'ah), when Allah looks upon His creation and grants forgiveness to all except polytheists and those who harbor hatred toward others. The teaching emphasizes the importance of maintaining pure faith and reconciling with others during this blessed night.

## Text Present:

**Header:** "The Prophet ﷺ said:"

**Arabic Text:**
إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَيَطّلِعُ فِي لَيْلَةِ النِّصْفِ مِنْ شَعْبَانَ، فَيَغْفِرُ لِجَمِيعِ خَلْقِهِ إِلَّا لِمُشْرِكٍ أَوْ مُشَاحِنِ

**English Translation:**
"Indeed Allah looks at His creation on the night of the middle of Sha'bān, and He forgives all of His creation except a polytheist or one who harbors hatred."

## Key Information:

This is a **Hadith** (saying of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ) regarding the spiritual significance of the **15th night of Sha'bān** (the Islamic month), also known as **Laylat al-Barā'ah** or the "Night of Forgiveness."

### Main Message:
The hadith emphasizes that:
- Allah's forgiveness is granted on this special night
- Forgiveness extends to all creation
- **Exceptions:** Those excluded are polytheists and those who harbor hatred/enmity toward others

This teaching highlights the importance of maintaining pure faith and reconciling with others during this blessed period.
