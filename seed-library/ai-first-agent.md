## Summary
A speaker from OpenAI reflects on the evolution of AI agents from early RL-based approaches like World of Bits to modern language model-driven systems, explaining why the field pivoted away from reinforcement learning. The talk emphasizes that while AI agents represent an important path to AGI, building them into practical products takes sustained effort, and draws parallels to neuroscience to inform future agent architecture design.
